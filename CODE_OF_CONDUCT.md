# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is committed to a respectful, constructive and harassment-free environment. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable and evidence-based feedback;
- respect different levels of experience and communication styles;
- accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes;
- protect confidential and personal information;
- keep project channels focused on the work and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention, publishing private information, sustained disruption and personal insults.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other spaces where a person is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments and other content that violates this standard. Responses may include a private warning, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion, depending on severity and recurrence.

For a sensitive report, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel and identify the submission as a conduct report. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and conflicts of interest will be avoided where possible.

Good-faith disagreement about technical or product decisions is not itself a violation. The manner of participation remains subject to this standard.
