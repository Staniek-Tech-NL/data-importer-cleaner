# Governance

Data Importer & Cleaner is currently maintained as a portfolio-led project by its repository owner.

## Decision making

- Product scope follows `docs/project-plan.md` and `docs/roadmap.md`.
- Business safety rules take precedence over convenience and visual polish.
- Significant technical decisions are recorded as ADRs under `docs/decisions`.
- The maintainer has final responsibility for roadmap, architecture, releases and community moderation.

## Contributions

Issues and pull requests are evaluated for product value, MVP fit, correctness, maintainability, tests, documentation and data safety. Acceptance of an idea does not guarantee scheduling.

## Transparency

Planned work is tracked through GitHub issues and milestones. Changes to public behavior should be described in pull requests and the changelog. Security and conduct reports remain private when disclosure could cause harm.

## Future governance

If sustained contributors join the project, maintainer roles, review responsibilities and release permissions will be documented here before authority is delegated.
